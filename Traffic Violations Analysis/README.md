## 🎯 **Object of the Analysis**

The objective of this analysis is to study the most common behaviors of individuals who violate traffic rules by addressing the following questions:

1️⃣ **What are the months with the highest violations?**

2️⃣ **What time do violations occur the most?**

3️⃣ **What is the most popular violation?**

4️⃣ **What is the most popular outcome of the violation?**

5️⃣ **What is the percentage of violators who get arrested?**

6️⃣ **Which gender commits the most speeding violations?**

7️⃣ **Which race commits the most violations?**

8️⃣ **Which age group is more likely to commit a violation?**

9️⃣ **What is the most frequent stop duration?**

---

## 🏁 **Conclusion**

| 💡 Insight                         | 🔍 Observation                                                                 |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Months with Highest Violations** | January has the highest number of violations, followed by November and October. |
| **Time of Violations**           | Most violations occur between 8 AM and 11 AM.                             |
| **Most Popular Violation**       | Speeding violations are the most common type.                             |
| **Most Popular Outcome**         | The majority of violation outcomes end with a Citation.                   |
| **Arrest Percentage**            | Only 3.7% of total violators end up getting arrested.                      |
| **Gender Analysis**              | Males commit the majority of speeding violations (68.6%), compared to females (31.4%). |
| **Race Analysis**                | White people account for 74.4% of total violations, followed by black people at 13.4%. |
| **Age Group Analysis**           | Violations are most common among individuals aged 20-30 years.            |
| **Stop Duration**                | Most stops due to violations last no longer than 15 minutes.              |
