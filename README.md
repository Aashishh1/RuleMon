#### 1. 🚦 Intersection Violation Detection
- Detects a variety of traffic violations including red light running, wrong-way driving, not following the directional sign, illegal lane change, weaving out of the lane, speeding, etc.

#### 2. 🟨 Yellow Box Junction Violation Detection
- Detects the violations of illegally driving into the yellow box junction of a crossroad when there is congestion
- Detects the violations of stranding or parking in the yellow box junction area near some important areas, such as schools, hospitals, etc.
- Provides abundant vehicle feature data – license plate number, vehicle color, manufacturer, type

#### 3. 🚧 Crossing the Gore Area Detection
- Detects gore area violations and records the whole violation process at the exit ramps of highways or complex urban road merging
- Provides abundant vehicle feature data – license plate number, vehicle color, manufacturer, type

| <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2a3d087c-ec04-4592-a4aa-ba5cebcd20e0" alt="Violation 3A" width="300" height="200"> | <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/976edd24-fea2-4528-a148-85a6133caf58" alt="Violation 3B" width="300" height="200"> |

#### 4. 🅿️ Illegal Parking Detection
- Reduced road occupancy by illegal parking
- Improves enforcement efficiency through video-based remote detection and management of parking violations
- Detects and reports illegally parked vehicles that can easily cause road congestion or traffic incidents at the roadside or bus stops
- Provides abundant vehicle feature data – license plate number, vehicle color, type
